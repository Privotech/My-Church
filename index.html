Welcome to Our Church

We are delighted to welcome you to "All Soul’s Winning for Saviour Global Ministry", a place where God's love, grace, and power are made manifest. Our mission is to win souls for Christ, disciple believers, and empower people to live victorious lives through the Word of God and the power of the Holy Spirit.

At ASWS Global Ministry, we believe that every soul matters to God, and it is our passion to spread the gospel to the ends of the earth. Whether you are joining us for the first time or are a committed member of our faith family, we want you to know that you are loved, valued, and welcome here.

Our Values

BEING SENT

Being sent means actively engaging with our local and global communities, meeting people where they are, and sharing the love and hope of Jesus through our words and actions.

J.O.Y

We seek to live out the transformative power of J.O.Y. – Jesus, Others, You. By prioritizing Jesus above all else, and then caring for others, we empower ourselves to better serve our community and grow in our faith walk.

WORSHIP

Our foundation is built on worship. We believe in the power of lifting our voices and hearts to God, celebrating His goodness, and encountering His presence.
